# Practice Tests

Choose **Practice Mode** for a focused review or **Certification Mode** for a timed examination. Both modes shuffle the questions and answer choices for each new attempt.

- [Launch A+ Core 2 Practice Test](app/index.html)

    Use the 104-question bank for focused practice or a 90-question, 90-minute MCQ-only certification simulation for CompTIA A+ Core 2 (220-1202).

## Practice Mode

Select domains, individual objectives, and a question count. The bank covers all four Core 2 domains and all 36 objectives, with at least two questions per objective.

Choose whether to see correct answers and detailed explanations. Explanations identify the correct answer, so they require correct-answer feedback. You can receive feedback after completing the test or after explicitly checking each answer. Once feedback is revealed, that response is locked.

## Certification Mode

Start a fixed examination with **90 questions and 90 minutes**. The timer begins when you select **Start Exam** and continues during navigation, whiteboard use, tab changes, and browser closure or refresh. The original deadline applies when you return.

The simulation selects 25 Operating Systems, 25 Security, 21 Software Troubleshooting, and 19 Operational Procedures questions, with broad objective coverage within each domain. The question count, domains, and feedback timing are fixed. Correct answers, explanations, and educational links are available after submission.

Skip questions, use **Flag for Review**, and return through the question navigator. You can change answers until submission. **Submit Exam** shows the answered, unanswered, and flagged totals and remaining time before asking for confirmation. At the deadline, the current responses are submitted automatically. Five-minute and one-minute notices do not interrupt your work.

This release is a **timed MCQ-only simulation**. The official examination can also include PBQs; the simulation does not reproduce its exact question mix or proprietary scoring.

## Notes, results, and saved progress

The shared **Whiteboard** is available in both modes for notes and normal copy and paste. It can move, resize, minimize, or dock where the screen supports those actions. Notes belong to the current attempt and do not affect scoring.

Single-answer questions have one correct choice. Multiple-response questions state how many choices to select and require the entire correct set for credit. Unanswered questions receive no credit. Results show a practice percentage and performance by domain and objective. Certification Mode also reports time used and whether you submitted manually or time expired. Review each question through the navigator.

Your attempt is saved locally in the same browser when browser storage is available. Refreshing restores its questions, answer order, selections, flags, notes, and current position. Certification Mode also restores its original deadline; an overdue exam opens as expired with results. If storage is blocked or full, the current in-memory attempt can continue, but refresh recovery is unavailable. Starting another attempt requires confirmation and clears the previous responses and notes. No account, subscription, or AI service is required.

Subtopic coverage and variety remain limited. Small samples cannot establish mastery. The questions are independently authored, and practice percentages are not CompTIA scaled scores or predictions of an official passing score.
