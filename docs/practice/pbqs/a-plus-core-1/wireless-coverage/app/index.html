The company occupies an approximately 3,000-square-foot office. Two wireless access points provide Wi-Fi throughout the workspace.

Most employees have reliable connectivity, but the employee in the northwest office reports a weak and unstable wireless connection.

Reposition the available equipment to improve wireless coverage throughout the office and resolve the reported connectivity problem.

How to place equipment
  1. Drag an access point to an empty position A–H, or select it and then select a position. Select the microwave and choose a breakroom surface to move it. Radios can be moved to available desks, counters, charging stations, or the sideboard.
  2. With the keyboard, use Tab to reach equipment and positions, then Enter or Space to select and place. Escape cancels a selection. Undo reverses the last move.
  3. Inspect equipment for details. Submit to check your design and view its coverage. Continue editing to try again. Reset restores the original office and assigns new starting positions to the radios.

Plan coverage around the workspace

Placement determines how well an access point serves the people using it.

Access point reach

An advertised coverage area assumes favorable conditions. Greater distance, interior walls, and building materials can reduce useful signal. Check the places where people actually work.

Shared coverage

Distribute access points so each serves a useful area. Some overlap supports movement between areas, but clustering devices can leave distant rooms with weak service.

Intermittent connections

Compare the times and locations of reported problems. Equipment that operates periodically can affect nearby wireless connections. Consider the operating band and nearby devices.
