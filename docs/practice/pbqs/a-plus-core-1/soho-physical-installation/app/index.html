How to build your installation
  1. Select equipment, then select an empty workspace position. Inspect the equipment to examine its sockets.
  2. Select a cable, select its first port, then select its second port. You can start at either end.
  3. Use the connection list to remove a lead. Undo reverses the last change. Escape cancels a selection.
  4. Submit when ready. Your choices are checked together, and you can edit and retry.

WAN and LAN

The WAN port connects a router to its provider-side Ethernet handoff. LAN ports serve local wired equipment. A switch adds local Ethernet ports; its ordinary ports are interchangeable for these connections.

Inspect the actual handoff

Provider service may arrive over coaxial cable, telephone wiring, or fiber. The service equipment presents a customer-facing connection, often Ethernet. Match the socket and cable at each end. Fiber equipment does not all use the same connector.
